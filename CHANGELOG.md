# Changelog

## 0.10.1

* In the Ember desktop app, Settings has a Server row and the sign-in page has a Change server link, so the server can be changed without finding the tray menu. Nothing changes in a browser.

## 0.10.0

* Ready for the Ember desktop app: inside it, new messages, nudges and calls show system notices and flash the taskbar, the unread count goes to the tray icon, and Settings has a Desktop alerts switch in place of the browser push row. In a browser nothing changes.
* The switches have the same measurements as the Android ones: the knob is centred when off, and has equal gaps at the top, bottom and right when on.

## 0.9.0

* Mentions, as in the Android app: typing @ in a group lists its members, narrows as you type, and puts in the name when you pick one. An @ inside an email address does not open the list. A name after @ in a message is highlighted, and yours stands out more. Arrow keys, Enter, Tab and Escape work in the list.
* Shared pictures, files and voice: the chat details have a list of everything shared in the chat, newest first. Pictures and videos in a grid by month, files and voice messages in a list, older ones on request. Sealed pictures open once encryption is set up in this browser.
* Archive from the chat list, as in the Android app: right click a chat, or press and hold on a phone, and choose Archive or Unarchive. No need to open the chat first.
* The picture viewer plays videos, and Escape in it closes only the viewer, not the sheet underneath.
* The sign-in page shows the Ember speech bubble and flame on its own in the accent colour, no circle or tile around it, as in the Android app.

## 0.8.3

* Nudge answers at once and every time, as in the Android app: your own chat shakes, the phone buzzes and the sound plays when you nudge, with no cool-down that made taps seem to do nothing. The sound is the real nudge recording the Hearth web app and the Android app use, not a beep.
* Message times take the colour of the message text at 60 percent, ticks keep their own colours, and a picture or video sent alone shows its time in a small dark pill on the picture, all as in the Android app.

## 0.8.2

* Nudge lines (and other room notices) sit among the messages by time, as in the Android app. New messages arrive below them instead of above.

## 0.8.1

* The Nudge button is in the top bar on every screen size, for everyone. Voice and video calls share one Call button that opens a small menu, as in the Android app, so the chat name has room.
* Signing in from an installed iPhone app: the sign-in is checked again for a moment if the cookie is slow to arrive, and requests now send cookies the same way the Hearth web app does.

## 0.8.0

* Spacing now matches the Android app: section headings, rows, icon gaps and the profile row use the same sizes in both.
* Version numbers follow the Android app: the last number for small fixes, the middle number for features, improvements and major fixes, the same release for both where they ship together.

## 0.7.0

* Wallpapers work like the big messengers: a flat colour that follows the theme and accent, a switch for doodles, and a dimming slider. Accent colour washes the flat colour. A photo can still be used.
* The doodles are now 84 large icons and 28 small shapes, placed off any grid, with no icon near a copy of itself. The Android app draws the same picture.
* The nudge button uses the same waving hand as the Android app, in the accent colour, instead of an emoji.
* Settings are regrouped: Calls sits next to Chats, GIFs and stickers after it, account and security together, About at the end. Shorter wording. Two columns when the screen is wide and short.

## 0.6.0

* The default wallpaper is a dense scatter in the way of the big messengers: 34 larger outline icons (camera, cup, headphones, umbrella, paw, gift and more) with small dots, rings and sparkles filling every gap. The same picture as in the Android app.

## 0.5.2

* The default wallpaper is jumbled and full: shapes sit off any grid, at different sizes, turned every way, with no empty patches. It repeats only every 1100 pixels.

## 0.5.1

* If a browser signs you in but does not keep the sign-in (iPhone: Block All Cookies, a private tab, or another app's built-in browser), Ember now says so and says what to change. Before, it showed only "Unauthorized".
* The wallpaper shapes are drawn as clean single strokes: no overlapping petals, no crossing lines.

## 0.5.0

* Signal sticker packs in the Stickers tab: search the public directory by what a pack is about, open a pack, tap a sticker to send it. The pictures come straight from Signal's servers.

## 0.4.1

* The doodle wallpaper has sixteen different shapes (stars, bolts, flowers, moons, notes, bubbles, leaves and more) in a wider repeat.

## 0.4.0

* Messages: a tap or click opens the actions. A long press picks several, with a bar for reply, copy, pin, edit, forward and delete. Swipe right to reply. Pictures and stickers have a forward button beside them.
* Search finds message text as well as chat names, in chats the server can read and, on this device, in sealed chats. A result opens at the message.
* Nudge button (in the attach menu on narrow screens), HD or Standard photo quality, and the six reactions you use most.
* Choose the message sound and the ringtone, or use a file of your own.
* Before a call: choose the microphone, camera and speaker, with a live microphone level.
* More room between bubbles, bolder and closer doodles on the default wallpaper.

## 0.3.0

* Appearance: Black theme for OLED screens, accent colours (nine presets or your own hex) and chat wallpapers (doodles, gradient, dots, plain or your own photo, with dimming), the same as the Android app.
* The GIF and sticker panel says so when the pictures of a service are blocked, and offers to try again.

## 0.2.0

* Emoji are the Apple pictures Hearth uses, in messages, reactions and the picker. Single emoji show large.
* Emoji, GIF and sticker panel under the message box. Favourites are kept in the browser. A sticker can be made from any picture or saved from a chat.
* Online GIFs and stickers from animated emoji, Reactions, Wikimedia, Giphy and Imgur.
* Calls: fit or fill per tile and for all, a scrolling grid for three or more people, tiles with clipped rounded corners, and a minimized call window so chats and people stay reachable.
* Fixed stickers being cropped inside their frame.

## 0.1.0

First release.

* Chats, groups, people, settings.
* Encrypted chats, attachments, voice notes.
* Calls with lobby, ring, screen share, battery warnings.
* Mirror and front camera flip options.
* PWA with push, offline shell and update prompt.
* Crash reports.
