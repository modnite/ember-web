# Changelog

## 0.6.0

* The default wallpaper is a dense scatter in the way of the big messengers: 34 larger outline icons (camera, cup, headphones, umbrella, paw, gift and more) with small dots, rings and sparkles filling every gap. The same picture as in the Android app.

## 0.5.2

* The default wallpaper is jumbled and full: shapes sit off any grid, at different sizes, turned every way, with no empty patches. It repeats only every 1100 pixels.

## 0.5.1

* If a browser signs you in but does not keep the sign-in (iPhone: Block All Cookies, a private tab, or another app's built-in browser), Ember now says so and says what to change. Before, it showed only "Unauthorized".
* The wallpaper shapes are drawn as clean single strokes: no overlapping petals, no crossing lines.

## 0.5.0

* Signal sticker packs in the Stickers tab: search the public directory by what a pack is about, open a pack, tap a sticker to send it. The pictures come straight from Signal's servers.

## 0.4.1

* The doodle wallpaper has sixteen different shapes (stars, bolts, flowers, moons, notes, bubbles, leaves and more) in a wider repeat.

## 0.4.0

* Messages: a tap or click opens the actions. A long press picks several, with a bar for reply, copy, pin, edit, forward and delete. Swipe right to reply. Pictures and stickers have a forward button beside them.
* Search finds message text as well as chat names, in chats the server can read and, on this device, in sealed chats. A result opens at the message.
* Nudge button (in the attach menu on narrow screens), HD or Standard photo quality, and the six reactions you use most.
* Choose the message sound and the ringtone, or use a file of your own.
* Before a call: choose the microphone, camera and speaker, with a live microphone level.
* More room between bubbles, bolder and closer doodles on the default wallpaper.

## 0.3.0

* Appearance: Black theme for OLED screens, accent colours (nine presets or your own hex) and chat wallpapers (doodles, gradient, dots, plain or your own photo, with dimming), the same as the Android app.
* The GIF and sticker panel says so when the pictures of a service are blocked, and offers to try again.

## 0.2.0

* Emoji are the Apple pictures Hearth uses, in messages, reactions and the picker. Single emoji show large.
* Emoji, GIF and sticker panel under the message box. Favourites are kept in the browser. A sticker can be made from any picture or saved from a chat.
* Online GIFs and stickers from animated emoji, Reactions, Wikimedia, Giphy and Imgur.
* Calls: fit or fill per tile and for all, a scrolling grid for three or more people, tiles with clipped rounded corners, and a minimized call window so chats and people stay reachable.
* Fixed stickers being cropped inside their frame.

## 0.1.0

First release.

* Chats, groups, people, settings.
* Encrypted chats, attachments, voice notes.
* Calls with lobby, ring, screen share, battery warnings.
* Mirror and front camera flip options.
* PWA with push, offline shell and update prompt.
* Crash reports.
