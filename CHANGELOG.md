# Changelog

## 0.2.0

* Emoji are the Apple pictures Hearth uses, in messages, reactions and the picker. Single emoji show large.
* Emoji, GIF and sticker panel under the message box. Favourites are kept in the browser. A sticker can be made from any picture or saved from a chat.
* Online GIFs and stickers from animated emoji, Reactions, Wikimedia, Giphy and Imgur.
* Calls: fit or fill per tile and for all, a scrolling grid for three or more people, tiles with clipped rounded corners, and a minimized call window so chats and people stay reachable.
* Fixed stickers being cropped inside their frame.

## 0.1.0

First release.

* Chats, groups, people, settings.
* Encrypted chats, attachments, voice notes.
* Calls with lobby, ring, screen share, battery warnings.
* Mirror and front camera flip options.
* PWA with push, offline shell and update prompt.
* Crash reports.
